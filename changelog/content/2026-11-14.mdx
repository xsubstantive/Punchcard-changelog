## Financial Statement Review

- **Whole-table prior-year comparison:** Financial Statement Review now lines up each table with the matching table from last year's statements, row by row and column by column. Figures that agree are marked as matched, and the rows that differ are flagged for you to look at.
- **Faster reviews of large statements:** More sections of a long statement are now reviewed at the same time. Narrative sections, notes, reports and primary statements get the most thorough review.
- **Less idle time on big files:** Reviewers that stop before finishing their pages now carry on without waiting for the rest of the review. Two reviewers also no longer work on the same pages at once.
- **Totals that add up are settled automatically:** A total the page labels as a total, which adds exactly, is recorded as checked with the reasoning and figures shown. Totals that don't add, and ones that might be a coincidence, still go to you.
- **More reliable saving on very large statements:** Saving a review of a very large statement, such as a several-hundred-page annual report, no longer fails.

## Analytics

- **Estimated time saved:** Analytics now estimates the preparation time saved from the citations recorded in your engagements. Review time is not included by default.
- **More accurate active-user counts:** A person now counts as active whenever they are signed in and using Punchcard. This applies to every firm, whatever its analytics consent settings. Work done by CoAudit on behalf of a task's owner is no longer counted as that person's own activity.
- **Faster Office citation tracking:** Recording citations from Excel and Word is lighter, which helps on engagements with a lot of citations.

## Clients

- **Optional avatar border color:** A client's avatar border color can now be set independently of the avatar itself.
