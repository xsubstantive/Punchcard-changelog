## Excel add-in

### Fixes

- **Citations follow your cells.** After you insert or delete cells with a shift, insert or delete table rows, or insert copied or cut cells, citations now move with the figures they refer to. Previously a citation could stay at its old address and point to a different number.
- **Works with the add-in closed.** Citations keep following moved cells whether the pane is open or closed, and for colleagues who don't have the add-in.
- **Deleted cells are clearly flagged.** If a cited cell is deleted, its citation shows an error. It no longer points to another cell.
- **Citation names stay out of sight.** Citation and reference names no longer show up in Name Manager or the Name Box after a workbook is updated. Workbooks already affected are tidied automatically. Your own named ranges are left alone.
- **Cut and paste keeps evidence.** If a cut-and-paste of cited cells can't be completed, the evidence on the original cells is kept and is no longer cleared.

### Performance

- **Large sheets stay responsive.** Adding citations across thousands of rows is much faster. A test with 10,000 rows dropped from about 6 seconds to a fraction of a second.
