- **Send scheduled client status reports.** Schedule reports with summaries, comments, attention badges, and highlighted client assignments. Recipients can unsubscribe in one click.

- **Copy evidence tables into Excel.** Copy and insert tables from supported evidence formats, including PDF tables with citations attached to individual cells.

- **Share Word files in External Review.** Word gains the read-only evidence-review experience available in Excel. Large Excel workbooks receive a warning instead of being refused outright.

- **Ask CoAudit to work beyond Excel cells.** Read and edit supported text boxes, comments, notes, charts, and pivot tables.

- **Review and revisit Word insights.** Browse saved responses, label insights, compare outcomes, and add citations to selected document passages.

- **Connect Word passages to evidence.** Internal passages can serve as citation evidence, with more reliable citation navigation and preservation of review decisions.

- **Choose the skills you use.** Manage skills from Customize on the web, opt into skills, and browse libraries named for your firm.
