## New

- **Adjustable pane zoom (Excel)**: Reviewers can now scale the CoAudit task pane down from the **Settings** page (the ⋯ menu in the pane header). Steps run from 100% down to 90%, 80%, 75%, 67%, and 50%. The preference is saved per device and applied before the pane loads, so the pane opens at the chosen size with no visible resize.

## Improvements

- **Consistent work status indicators**: Status icons and colors across Punchcard have been unified into a coherent set, making it easier to read work status at a glance across different parts of the app.

## Bug Fixes

- **Chat citation highlights stay visible when clicking other cells (Excel)**: Clicking a citation link in a CoAudit chat response opens the source document with the relevant passage highlighted. Previously, clicking any workbook cell without its own linked evidence would clear that highlight. The highlight from a chat citation now persists until you click a cell that has its own evidence, or the viewer resets.
- **CoAudit's cell and sheet references stay accurate within a turn (Excel)**: CoAudit now locks in the cell selection and active worksheet at the moment you press **Send**, and uses that snapshot consistently across every step it takes in that response — including when it navigates between sheets during its work. This prevents CoAudit from referencing the wrong cell or sheet mid-turn. The same fix applies to queued follow-up messages, each of which carries the selection captured when it was queued.
