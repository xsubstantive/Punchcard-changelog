## New

- **TIFF files now open in the viewer** — TIFF images, including multi-page files, display directly in the workpaper viewer. Every page is scanned for text, so you can lasso selections, place citations, and search TIFF content just like any other document.

## Add-in improvements

- **Stay signed in longer** — The add-in now keeps you signed in for up to 14 days of inactivity and up to 90 days in total. You no longer need to re-authenticate after a day off or a long weekend.
- **Jump to any level in the engagement picker** — The firm and client names at the top of the engagement picker are now tappable. Tapping opens a menu showing the full navigation path — for example, My Engagements → All Clients — so you can jump directly to any level without stepping back through each screen.
- **Browse all clients from My Engagements** — An "All clients" link now appears in the My Engagements view, letting you see the full client list without navigating away first.
- **Context tab leads with "Browse Punchcard"** — When no files are attached, the primary action in the Context tab is now "Browse Punchcard," making it one tap to pull in the engagement's evidence.
