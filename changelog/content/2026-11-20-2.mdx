## Improvements

- **Keyboard range selection from checkboxes**: In the Files tab and Library, you can now use Space to toggle a document's checkbox, and hold Shift with the arrow keys, Home, or End to extend your selection while keeping focus on the checkboxes.

## Fixes

- **Select mode in a Request keeps your earlier picks**: Extending a selection with Shift and the arrow keys, or with a modified click on a filename, now adds to what you already chose instead of replacing it.
- **Ticking a Request now counts what is inside it**: In the Context tab's Select mode, ticking a Request along with files inside it now shows the right count and keeps **Remove from context** available, instead of wrongly reporting a larger count and blocking removal.
- **Sub-request documents are no longer double counted**: A document from a sub-request that is ticked together with its parent Request is now covered by that Request.
- **Focus stays put while scrolling long lists**: Controls keep keyboard focus when long file lists reveal new rows.
