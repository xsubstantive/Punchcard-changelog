## Bug Fixes

- **Date variables can now be created without losing the form.** In Engagement Settings, when adding a new variable with the Date type, opening the calendar to select a value previously dismissed the entire form — making it impossible to save a date variable. The form now stays open while you pick a date and choose a format, so you can complete and save the variable without starting over.
