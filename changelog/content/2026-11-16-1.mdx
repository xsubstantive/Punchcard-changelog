## Financial Statement Review

- **Lite review now records what agrees, not only what's wrong.** Along with flagging problems, Financial Statement Review lite mode now confirms the figures that tie out. It checks three kinds of agreement: ties to other places in the statement, totals and ratios, and comparatives against last year's statement.
- **Much closer to the full review's coverage.** In testing on six graded statements, lite review now matches about 77% of the full review's tick marks, up from 37%.
- **Small trade-off in time.** Lite review takes roughly 20–90 seconds longer per statement.
- **Every confirmation can be re-performed.** Each agreement is recorded as a check that the full review can re-perform, so you can follow the support behind it.
