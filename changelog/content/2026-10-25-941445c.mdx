---
title: "Create and manage sub-requests"
description: "Punchcard adds sub-request workflows and better protects edits and evidence while organizing request tables."
date: "2026-10-25"
tags: ["Feature", "Bug Fix"]
version: "2026.10.25"
---

- **Break requests into sub-requests.** Create, view, and manage sub-requests within an engagement.

- **Keep your edits when reorganizing tables.** Undo and table conversions better preserve changes made to sub-requests.

- **Protect work shared by several people.** Simultaneous edits and evidence matching are preserved more reliably.

- **Browse requests from the add-in.** Requests can be selected when browsing for evidence in the Office pane.
