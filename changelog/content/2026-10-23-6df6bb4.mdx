- **Navigate a simpler engagement board.** Updated tabs, filters, file access, period selection, and status reports bring engagement work into a flatter layout.

- **See comments by default.** Comments are visible in the default board view, with an option to reset saved views.

- **Use audit functions directly in Excel.** Worksheet functions cover engagement data, evidence, sampling, analytics, and exchange rates.

- **Recover from an ended sign-in.** The web app takes you to sign-in promptly, while the Office pane stays in place.

- **Understand why a sheet cannot be added.** CoAudit explains Excel’s refusal and offers a fallback.
