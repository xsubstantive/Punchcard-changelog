### Bug Fixes

- **Excel & Word add-in — sign-in loop for new accounts**: Fixed an issue where a user whose account had just been activated could get stuck in an unescapable cycle: the add-in would show a sign-in pop-up, the pop-up would detect a valid session and close itself automatically, then the whole sequence would repeat. Affected accounts now move directly into the add-in after sign-in. If a session has genuinely lapsed, the add-in displays a clear prompt to sign out and back in — no loop.
