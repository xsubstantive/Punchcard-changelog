## Fixes

- **No more duplicate notification summaries.** If you get notification summaries two or more times a day, a summary delivered shortly before its scheduled time (for example, just before 9 AM) could be sent again at that scheduled time. Summaries now always move on to the next upcoming time, so you get one summary per slot.
- **Summaries restart correctly after changes.** This also applies when you turn email notifications back on, when a mention triggers delivery of a summary that was already due, and when a new person's first summary schedule is set up.
- **Schedules that don't divide evenly are covered.** Summary times now work correctly for any number of summaries per day, including ones that don't fall on whole hours.
