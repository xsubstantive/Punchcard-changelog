## Financial Statement Review (beta)

- **Choose and save your review scope.** Pick which statements and sections a review covers, and the scope is saved with the review.
- **Rebuilt findings panel.** Findings are easier to scan, with category tick marks on the statement pages. Each finding states its reasoning, including any open points, instead of posing separate questions.
- **Comment threads on findings.** Discuss a finding with your team in a thread. When a finding is dismissed, its thread is answered with the supporting evidence.
- **Progress in chat.** See how far each part of the review has got while it runs.
- **Sharper presentation checks.** The review keeps clear typos, however minor, and leaves out style preferences and accepted variants. Each underlying issue is recorded once, with every place it appears.
- **Better statement recognition.** Statements named by the items they list, such as "Assets, Liabilities, and Members' Equity", are now identified correctly.
- **Financial statement review (beta – lite).** A faster review option that reads every page once, at the same time, against the whole statement. Start it from Create or from a beta review.

## Improvements

- **Clearer Request wording.** Copy link, Create, the status report, the members dialog and sub-requests now say "request" where they mean a client Request. The status report's withheld count reads, for example, "2 in Draft".
- **More accurate client invitations.** The invitation email counts only the Requests that are ready for the client to review, leaving out internal tasks and Drafts.
- **Consistent checkboxes and switches.** Checkboxes share one style, and switches are more compact.
- **Hover hints on engagement tabs.** Engagement tabs show tooltips in place of the browser's default titles.

## Fixes

- The statement chooser now closes after you save a beta review's first statements from its page.
- The review page has its Back button again.
- A finding's accessible name includes its figure's label.
- Clicking a comment keeps it in place when a thread above it collapses.
- The Ask option now waits for the review's details to load, so an already started lite review isn't treated as a first run.
