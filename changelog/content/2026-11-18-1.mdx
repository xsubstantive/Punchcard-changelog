## AI

- **GPT-6.2 Sol is now available in CoAudit.** Open the model and effort picker in the chat composer and choose GPT-6.2 Sol. It appears first in the model list and works at every effort level from Low through Max.
- **Your default doesn't change.** CoAudit still answers with GPT-6.1 Sol at Medium effort unless you pick a different model. Your choice is saved per chat, and "Reset to default" returns you to the recommended setup.
