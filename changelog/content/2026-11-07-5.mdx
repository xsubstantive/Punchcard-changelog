## Fixes

- **Citation previews open the right source.** When you clicked one citation and then quickly clicked another, a slow lookup for the first could finish late and open its preview over the second. Now only the citation you clicked last opens its preview. A slow earlier lookup no longer covers it or shows a stray "couldn't find" message.
