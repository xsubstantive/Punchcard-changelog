## Features

- **Redesigned Clients workspace** — Browse your clients and their engagements in a single searchable outline. Select a client to view and edit its details and appearance, or select an engagement to manage its settings and team, all without leaving the page. Keyboard navigation and multi-select make it faster to work across engagements.
- **Create from anywhere** — A single Create menu on both Home and the Clients page lets you add a new client or engagement right where you are, without navigating to a separate page.
- **Profile photos** — Upload, change, or remove your own profile photo from the account menu. Your photo now appears throughout Punchcard in place of your initials.

## Improvements

- **Optional audit periods** — Audit period dates are no longer required when creating or updating an engagement. If you set just a start or end date, the other is now filled in automatically as a one-year period.
- **Request inspector** — A new menu next to a request's title in the quick-view panel keeps the "Open workpaper" / "Add workpaper" action out of the way until you need it.
- **Home tips** — First-visit tips now show your progress through the set and let you skip all remaining tips at once.

## Fixes

- Unresolved comment counts and filters on a task's activity stream no longer include automatically generated system notes, matching the counts shown on the board.
