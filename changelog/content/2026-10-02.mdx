### Features & Improvements

- **Redesigned task and request creation**: The "New Task" / "New Request" modal has been restyled for a cleaner, faster creation flow. Client and engagement names now appear immediately when opened from a board (no blank placeholder while loading), the form can be **expanded** for longer descriptions, and you can submit with **⌘+Enter** (Mac) or **Ctrl+Enter** (Windows) from anywhere in the form.
- **Easier template browsing**: The "Use a template" picker (on the web and in the Excel add-in) now has **All / Punchcard / Firm / Personal** filter tabs, each showing how many templates match your search — making it faster to find the right one.
- **CoAudit in Excel handles large data imports more reliably**: CoAudit can now write large sets of calculated results (hundreds of rows) into a workbook in one pass instead of retyping them value-by-value, significantly improving accuracy on big imports. It can also read text from scanned documents more thoroughly and view PDF pages — including filled-in form fields — when researching your files.
- **Longer CoAudit tasks in Excel no longer get cut off**: Bulk data imports, multi-step reviews, and other long-running CoAudit jobs in the Excel add-in now run to completion instead of stopping partway through after about 15 minutes.

### Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue where **filled-in form fields and annotations** in PDF documents (typed values, checkboxes, highlights, stamps) appeared blank in the document viewer, even though that information was already available elsewhere. These now render correctly.
- **Task chat history** now shows chats that teammates started on the same task, not just your own conversations — restoring visibility that was previously lost.
- Fixed a rare issue where a task's **privacy toggle** could silently revert to its previous state shortly after being changed.
