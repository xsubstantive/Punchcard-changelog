- **Automatically submit on upload.** Opt in to have a request submitted when files are uploaded.

- **Preserve urgent priorities from Suralink.** Requests imported with Very High priority become Urgent in Punchcard.

- **Use image tickmarks in Excel.** Excel workflows support image-based tickmarks.

- **More reliable client links and CoAudit responses.** Certain client addresses resolve correctly, and affected CoAudit conversations no longer fail while returning a response.
