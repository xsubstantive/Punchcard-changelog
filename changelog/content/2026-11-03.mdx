## Features

- **Live tables can pull in rows from Request follow-ups.** When a Request has follow-up items, the evidence returned for them can now feed rows into a live table. This saves you from copying the data over by hand.
- **Computed columns are protected.** Rows collected this way no longer overwrite columns that Punchcard calculates for you.
- **Source references carry through.** Each collected row keeps its link to the source before any new column headers are added.

## Fixes

- **Tabs keep their glide animation when you switch pages.** The tab highlight no longer jumps or restarts partway through its movement when the page reloads underneath it.
- **Tab buttons stay visible while you press them.** On touch screens, a tab no longer disappears under your finger or looks broken during a tap.
