- **Add individual documents to workbook context.** Include single evidence files alongside folders.

- **Choose what to include from sub-requests.** Select the relevant parts of a request and see them nested in Context.

- **Avoid duplicate context.** Adding a folder replaces separate context entries for the documents and folders it already contains.

- **Share citations without reasoning.** Read-only review warns before removing reasoning and presents citations for the recipient.

- **Get started in Excel and Word.** A guided Agent introduction appears after sign-in in the Office panes.

- **Use a consistent profile photo.** Uploaded photos appear wherever Punchcard shows your avatar.

- **Choose your celebration style.** Personal preferences let you control success moments.
