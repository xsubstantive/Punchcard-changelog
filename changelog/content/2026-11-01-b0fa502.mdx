- **Create projects wherever you work.** Make projects and start chats inside them from the narrow web pane and the Excel and Word panes.

- **Follow Word evidence from chat.** CoAudit answers can cite document content and link back to the relevant passage.

- **Add overlapping citations together.** CoAudit can place overlapping Word citations in a single edit.

- **Protect pasted citations.** Word refuses affected edits when a pasted citation cannot be safely distinguished from its original.
