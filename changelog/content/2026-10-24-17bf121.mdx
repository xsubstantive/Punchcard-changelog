- **Ask CoAudit to organize your worksheet.** Group, collapse, and expand rows and columns with Excel’s outline controls.

- **Work with cell borders.** CoAudit can read, set, and match borders, with Undo restoring the original formatting.

- **Choose whether citation highlights are visible.** Turn highlights off and back on while preserving deliberate cell fills.

- **Organize workbook uploads.** Uploads suggest a folder named after the workbook, which you can rename before it is created.

- **Jump to the cited row.** Citations into large Excel sheets land on the relevant row more reliably.

- **Recover more smoothly in the Office pane.** Reconnecting the pane and reading back after interrupted edits are more dependable.

- **Clearer worksheet errors.** Edits to renamed or missing sheets identify the affected sheet and its new name when known.
