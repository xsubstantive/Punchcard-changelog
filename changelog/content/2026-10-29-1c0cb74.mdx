- **Send a workbook with its evidence.** Review workbooks can carry their supporting documents for read-only review without signing in, with a way to make them live again.

- **Keep request numbers on import.** Imported request lists retain their own numbering.

- **Choose how to renumber.** Number requests and internal tasks together or separately; the All view follows the order shown.

- **Change a request number with less disruption.** Choose any available number; a conflict shifts numbering only as far as the nearest gap.

- **Collect shared sub-request folders.** Choose folders shared by sub-requests directly from collection and source controls.

- **Compare ratios with percentages correctly.** Manual calculation checks treat stated percentages on the right scale.
