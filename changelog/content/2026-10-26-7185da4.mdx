description: "Punchcard introduces firm-based addresses and improves navigation, linked files, and Word review reliability."

---

- **Recognizable engagement addresses.** Links include the firm, client, and engagement context, helping distinguish engagements that share an identifier.

- **Existing links keep their destination.** Older links preserve the intended document and panel; ambiguous links offer choices you are allowed to open.

- **Move between engagements faster.** Recent engagements and the board you are about to open are prepared ahead of time.

- **Keep chat uploads with the current engagement.** Uploaded chat files save within the engagement you are working in.

- **Protect Word review work.** User edits are preserved more reliably, and review-save failures are shown instead of passing unnoticed.
