## CoAudit works in Office on the web

The CoAudit by Punchcard add-in now works in **browser-based Excel and Word** — opened through office.com, SharePoint, or OneDrive. Previously, signing in through the Office web app would result in a redirect loop; the add-in now authenticates correctly in browser-hosted workbooks, giving you the full CoAudit experience without needing the desktop app.

- When sign-in cannot complete — for example, in private or incognito windows, or in browser configurations that block third-party cookies — a clear **"Sign-in could not complete"** screen now explains the issue and provides a **Try again** link, instead of a confusing browser error page.

## Excel add-in: recovery when Office stops responding during load

If Excel refuses or stops responding while the CoAudit pane is starting up, the pane now **detects the failure within 15 seconds** and shows a recovery screen instead of staying blank.

- The recovery screen names **exactly which step failed** and whether Excel refused the request or stopped responding — so you know what happened and what to do next.
- The screen confirms that your **citations and sign-offs in the workbook were not changed**.
- A **Try again** button re-runs the complete pane startup so you can recover without restarting Excel.
- While waiting, a **CoAudit chat** opens beneath the recovery screen so you can continue asking questions even when the workbook is temporarily unreachable.

## Fixes

- On shared Office profiles, **signing out now clears CoAudit chat history** so the next user starts from a clean state rather than seeing the previous user's conversation.
