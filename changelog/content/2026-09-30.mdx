**Sub-requests for breaking down PBC requests**
- Split any request into individually trackable sub-requests — add them one at a time, or create a batch by pasting a table (one sub-request per row) using the same sampling options available elsewhere in Punchcard.
- Accepting a request with open sub-requests offers to accept all of them at once, with an Undo if you change your mind.
- Move a sub-request to a different request, detach it as its own standalone request, or reorder sub-requests by typing a new position.
- A sampling receipt (Excel and PDF) is available whenever sub-requests were created from a sampled table.

**Clearer client submission controls**
- Clients now see a single, focused status control: Submit for review, Withdraw a submission, or Reopen an accepted request — each applies instantly with an Undo option instead of a confirmation dialog.
- Adding a comment or uploading a file to an accepted request automatically sends it back for review, with a clear notice beforehand and Undo afterward.
- After a client uploads files to a request that hasn't been submitted yet, a gentle nudge offers to submit it for review right away.

**Redesigned Home for auditors**
- Home now shows your engagements in a sortable, filterable list (Active, Archived, or All) with search, grouping by client or status, and progress at a glance.
- Expand any engagement row to see an unread-first activity feed for that engagement.
- Clients who belong to more than one engagement now get their own Home to switch between them.
- A "Latest from our changelog" column keeps you up to date on what's new in Punchcard without leaving Home.

**Engagement Activity tab**
- A new Activity tab on the engagement board shows the full history of comments, file uploads, and status changes across every request and task — filterable by Everyone, Client, or Team, with an unread count and a "mark all read" option.

**CoAudit understands what's on your screen**
- CoAudit now picks up on what you have open or selected — the file and page you're viewing, the tasks selected on a board, the statement or checklist item in Financial Statement Review — so follow-ups like "this file" or "this finding" just work without re-explaining.
- CoAudit can also look up and act on a different task, engagement, or client by name from any chat, not just the one currently on screen, and always confirms before changing something you're not looking at.

**Financial Statement Review**
- Rotate pages that were scanned sideways directly from the viewer's zoom controls, so landscape documents display right-side up.

**Client organizations**
- Auditors can now upload a custom image for a client, or choose any color for their initials avatar.
