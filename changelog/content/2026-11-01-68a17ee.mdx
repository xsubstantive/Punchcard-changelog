---
title: "Stay signed in longer while actively using Punchcard"
description: "Punchcard extends the maximum active sign-in period to 180 days while retaining the inactivity limit."
date: "2026-11-01"
tags: ["Feature"]
version: "2026.11.01"
---

- **Fewer repeat sign-ins during ongoing work.** Active sign-ins can last up to 180 days from the original sign-in.

- **Inactive sign-ins still expire.** The 14-day inactivity limit remains in place.
